# ChargerInstallerList Voice Profile

## Voice summary
Practical research for homeowners who need a Level 2 charger installed by an electrician who actually shows EV-charging work. We separate installers from public charging stations, networks, equipment sellers, and generic electrical listings so a buyer can call someone useful.

## Core traits
- Practical filter: remove station pins and equipment-only dead ends.
- Electrically literate: explain load calculations, panel capacity, hardwiring, receptacles, and load management in plain language.
- Brand-agnostic: Tesla, ChargePoint, Wallbox, Emporia, Enphase, Autel, FLO, or another listed unit.
- Evidence-bounded: website and EV-specific review signal, not an endorsement or license check.
- Audience-aware: homeowner copy emphasizes panel capacity, permits, and equipment choice; commercial copy emphasizes utility coordination, load management, accessibility, networking, and commissioning.

## Preferred language
- "someone who has installed an EV charger before"
- "EV-charger signal"
- "Level 2 charging"
- "load calculation"
- "panel capacity"
- "hardwired or receptacle-connected"
- "confirm licensing, permits, and commissioning"

## Avoid
- "trusted network", "vetted pros", "certified partner" without primary evidence
- implying every EV charger requires a panel upgrade
- calling public charging-station locations installers
- treating an open breaker slot as proof of electrical capacity
- em dashes and AI filler
