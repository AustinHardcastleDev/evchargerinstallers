# 06 — Equipment warranty and networking

**Verified as of:** 2026-08-11

## Executive summary

Buy ENERGY STAR or NRTL-listed equipment where possible. Outdoor installs need outdoor-rated gear.
Smart Level 2 units may collect data, schedule charging, and require network setup. Warranty
responsibility can split between equipment maker and installer labor; get that in writing.

## Detailed findings

- AFDC recommends safety-certified equipment (e.g., ENERGY STAR / nationally recognized testing lab).
- Advanced Level 2 products may include timers, communications, and user interfaces.
- Multifamily/commercial networking often includes access control, load sharing, and payment
  backends that residential garage installs do not need.

## Claims we can make

- "Prefer listed/certified EVSE and outdoor-rated equipment for outdoor locations."
- "Confirm who warrants equipment versus installation labor."
- "Networked chargers may need Wi-Fi, Ethernet, or cellular; include that in scope."

## Claims to avoid

- Lifetime or "future-proof" warranty claims without primary docs.
- Treating installer website "certified" language as manufacturer authorization.

## Known gaps

- Brand warranty terms change; cite OEM pages when naming a brand.

## Sources

- Manufacturer warranty/install docs (as cited per guide)
