# 07 — Choosing an installer

**Verified as of:** 2026-08-11

## Executive summary

Look for clear EV-charger installation evidence on the contractor's own site or EV-specific reviews.
Confirm electrical licensing for the jurisdiction. Treat manufacturer "certified" website claims as
claims until matched to an official directory. EVITP certifies electricians; contractors in the
EVITP directory are typically described as EVITP Approved.

## Detailed findings

- This directory's inclusion claim is website/review signal, not vetting or endorsement.
- Credential language must stay hedged unless an official directory exact match exists.
- Useful buyer checks: license, load calculation, amperage/circuit sizing, hardwired vs receptacle,
  panel/service vs load management, permit responsibility, commissioning, outdoor rating, warranty.

## Claims we can make

- "Ask for the load calculation and the proposed breaker/circuit size in writing."
- "Confirm who pulls the permit and meets the inspection."
- "Website mentions of certification are claims until matched to a current official directory."
- "EVITP Approved describes contractors in EVITP's contractor directory; EVITP Certified applies to
  electricians unless an official source says otherwise."

## Claims to avoid

- "Trusted," "vetted," "premier," "verified installer."
- "EVITP-certified contractor" as a company credential.
- Treating Google star ratings as our endorsement.

## Known gaps

- Official manufacturer directories are not all scrape-safe; enrichment is optional.
