# 10 — State and regional variation

**Verified as of:** 2026-08-11

## Executive summary

NEC edition, licensing, and permitting are state/local. Housing form and electricity rates vary
widely. This brief holds the 50-state research table. Zero numeric fields mean not populated yet
and must not appear in public copy.

## Detailed findings

- Permits remain local even under statewide code adoption.
- Cold-climate outdoor installs emphasize weatherproofing and cord management; dense coastal metros
  emphasize multifamily/shared parking.
- Do not invent city facts; only add metro-named mentions when a primary source names the
  jurisdiction.

## Claims we can make

- "Confirm the adopted electrical code edition and who may pull the permit for the job address."
- Statewide statistics only when the table row is populated with a year and source URL.

## Claims to avoid

- Publishing zero placeholders.
- City EV ordinances without a citation naming that city.

## Known gaps

- Census ACS / EIA API keyed refresh needed for housing and rate columns.
- NEC edition and board licensing text need per-state primary pages.
