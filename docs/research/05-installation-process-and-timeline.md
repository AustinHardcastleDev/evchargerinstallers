# 05 — Installation process and timeline

**Verified as of:** 2026-08-11

## Executive summary

A typical residential process is: site assessment and load calculation, equipment selection,
permit, circuit/run installation, mounting, inspection, and commissioning (including any network
setup). Timelines are local because permit queues and utility coordination vary. Commercial and
multifamily jobs add ownership, parking, utility, and accessibility steps.

## Detailed findings

- Pre-purchase planning should include parking location, indoor/outdoor rating, amperage, and
  whether hardwiring or a receptacle is in scope.
- Commissioning may include app pairing, Wi-Fi/Ethernet, access control, or utility metering for
  commercial sites.
- Trenching or long conduit runs dominate schedules when the panel is far from the parking spot.

## Claims we can make

- "Expect a site visit or detailed photo/plan review before a firm quote."
- "Permit and inspection steps are local; ask who pulls the permit."
- "Commissioning and network setup should be explicit in the scope of work when the equipment is
  networked."

## Claims to avoid

- National "install in one day" promises.
- Guaranteed permit turnaround times.

## Known gaps

- No national timeline dataset; keep language principle-first.
