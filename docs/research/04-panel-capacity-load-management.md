# 04 — Panel capacity and load management

**Verified as of:** 2026-08-11

## Executive summary

An open breaker slot is not proof of capacity. A load calculation determines whether a Level 2
circuit can be added. Listed load-management / energy-management systems can sometimes avoid or
defer a service upgrade by sharing capacity with other loads. This is a engineering decision, not a
marketing slogan.

## Detailed findings

- AFDC: electricians can determine whether a home has adequate capacity; some homes need added
  circuits or service work for Level 2.
- Continuous-load treatment increases required ampacity relative to non-continuous loads.
- Load management products must be listed for the application; "smart charger scheduling" alone is
  not the same as service-level energy management.

## Claims we can make

- "A load calculation, not an empty panel slot, determines whether a Level 2 charger can be added."
- "Load management can sometimes avoid or defer a service upgrade when listed equipment and a proper
  design are used."
- "Ask the electrician to document the load calculation and any energy-management approach in the
  quote."

## Claims to avoid

- "Every home needs a panel upgrade for Level 2."
- "A 200 A panel automatically has capacity."
- Guaranteeing no service upgrade without a site assessment.

## Known gaps

- Product-specific EMS listing details change; cite manufacturer manuals when naming a product.
